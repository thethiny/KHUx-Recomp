     the rewrite side is now a mismatch ("RW FAULT", the original's result is kept). Seen: sub_68524A, SceneLight::update,
     cocos2d::CallFunc::update, CarouselDeck::addMedalOntoDeck (a host address outside guest memory; did not come
     back in 3 one-quest sessions, verified by a direct call), EnemyActor::addDamage (fixed 2026-10-23: it decoded the
     defeated flag into a host local - SafeBuffer::read stores into guest memory). The RW FAULT line now names the
     host frames (module+offset; rewrite/tools/mapaddr.py resolves rewrite.dll offsets).
  The older bad_alloc (cocostudio DataReaderHelper::addDataFromFile builds a string from getFileData's size, unchecked)
  and LWFNode::create null crashes did not come back in three full sessions; if they do, the fopen-failure line in
